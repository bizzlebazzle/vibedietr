Your VibeDietr account export — schema 1.1.0

Extract this ZIP and open index.html for offline navigation and counts.
manifest.json identifies this edition and the generated file inventory.
schema/account-export-1.1.0.schema.json and schema/data-dictionary.md describe
the exact machine representation. Open data/*.json in a UTF-8 text editor or
JSON reader. No network connection, script or remote asset is needed.

This is portable account content, not an application backup or a promise of
application restore or third-party import. Generation reads committed database
content during one transaction, after your request; later edits are excluded.

Decimals are exact stored strings, never display-rounded numbers. IDs join
records by type and are installation-scoped. Your account is self. External
content references are provenance, not permission or guaranteed live links.
Outgoing recipients have archive-local aliases without account identities.
Null means missing/not applicable; empty text and arrays remain distinct from
zero. Trace/limits retain their recorded status. Calculated values are estimates.
Dates and local consumption time, timezone, offset and UTC history are retained.

The archive excludes credentials, foreign account data, restricted security,
audit and moderation evidence, transient files, source URLs/internal import
references and raw provider payloads. Owned stored wording and pinned
snapshots remain intact. See the data dictionary for details and counting units.
Your download expires seven days after the request. Keep any downloaded copies
private; expiry cannot delete copies you have already downloaded.
